FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Meta 360 Campaign Insights

What is the minimum ad budget required to work with Vijayho on Meta?

We typically recommend a minimum monthly ad budget of ₹30,000 to ₹50,000 for local campaigns and ₹1,00,000+ for pan-India brand scaling. This provides sufficient conversion data for Meta's AI algorithm to exit the learning phase and optimize for the lowest cost per lead.

How does Conversions API (CAPI) solve tracking issues caused by iOS 14+?

Browser-based pixels run on the client device and are frequently blocked by Safari, iOS privacy settings, and ad-blockers. Our server-side CAPI pipeline sends purchase and lead events directly from your cloud server to Meta's servers, restoring 100% of lost conversion data and lowering your customer acquisition costs.

Can Meta Ads generate real B2B leads for Surat textile and diamond businesses?

Yes! We specialize in Surat's commercial ecosystem. By pairing high-authority video reels with direct WhatsApp Click-to-Chat automation and instant catalog downloads, we generate high-intent inquiries from retailers, distributors, and buyers across India daily.
