Mobile App Insights

Do you publish the app under our own company Apple & Google accounts?

Yes. You retain 100% intellectual property and ownership. We assist you in setting up your Apple Developer Program ($99/yr) and Google Play Console ($25 one-time) accounts and handle the end-to-end signing, certificates, and compliance.

How do you ensure our mobile app doesn't crash during flash sales or heavy traffic?

We implement Firebase Crashlytics, automated unit testing, Sentry telemetry, and serverless auto-scaling backends. Our state machines and memory pools prevent memory leaks, maintaining a 99.98% crash-free session rate even at massive concurrency.
